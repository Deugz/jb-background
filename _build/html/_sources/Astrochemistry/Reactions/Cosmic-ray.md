# Cosmic Ray-Induced

## Cosmic Ray Ionization

Cosmic rays can ionize molecules in the ISM, leading to the formation of ions that can then participate in further reactions.
     
- Example: 

$$
\text{H}_2 + \text{CR} \rightarrow \text{H}_2^+ + e^-
$$   
   
## Secondary Photodissociation

Cosmic rays can excite hydrogen molecules, which then emit UV photons that can dissociate other molecules nearby.
     
- Example: 


$$ 
\text{CO} + h\nu_{sec} \rightarrow \text{C} + \text{O} 
$$

where  $h\nu_{sec}$  is a secondary photon produced by cosmic ray excitation
