# Photodissociation and Photoionization

## Photodissociation 

This occurs when a molecule absorbs a photon and breaks apart into smaller fragments. This is a common process in regions exposed to UV light, such as the surfaces of molecular clouds.
     
- Example: 

$$
\text{CO} + h\nu \rightarrow \text{C} + \text{O}
$$

## Photoionization 

A molecule or atom absorbs a photon with enough energy to eject an electron, resulting in an ion.
     
- Example: 

$$
\text{H}_2 + h\nu \rightarrow \text{H}_2^+ + e^-
$$
